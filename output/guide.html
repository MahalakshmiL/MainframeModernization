CP-04 · Hogan Estate · Deposit / Account Servicing

What the mainframe programs do

A simplified deposit and account-servicing module: six COBOL programs running on a mainframe, from a teller posting a single deposit to the nightly batch run that charges fees, closes dormant accounts, and prints statements — plus a seventh program, overdraft protection, written in PL/I rather than COBOL, the way real mainframe estates often mix languages by subsystem. What follows is a plain-English account of what each program actually does and the business rules inside it — read from the live code paragraph by paragraph, not from documentation that may have gone stale.

Three COBOL programs are online, run ad hoc in any order whenever a transaction comes in: ACCTINQ, DEPOSIT, WITHDRWL. Three run as a nightly batch in a fixed sequence set by the JCL: ACCTMSTR, then INTCALC, then STMTGEN. All six read and write the same two shared files — ACCOUNT-FILE and TRANSACTION-FILE — and the batch's output is unloaded nightly into DB2. OVRDRAFT, the PL/I program, runs independently of all six and doesn't touch those files.

What it accomplishes

The programs are the mechanism. This is the job they do.

  • Lets a customer move money — deposit into or withdraw from their account, with guardrails: a closed account can't be touched, a withdrawal can't push the balance past a $100 overdraft cushion, and a dormant account is automatically reactivated the moment a deposit comes in rather than requiring a separate "unfreeze" step.
  • Rewards savers, charges accounts that run low — savings balances earn monthly interest, with a bonus rate once a balance clears $10,000; checking and savings accounts below a minimum balance are charged a monthly maintenance fee instead of earning anything.
  • Winds down abandoned accounts on its own — an account with no activity for 180 days is flagged dormant, and after a full year with still no activity it's closed outright, so nobody has to manually notice and act on accounts customers have walked away from.
  • Backstops honest overdrafts instead of just bouncing them — the PL/I program decides, per request, whether a withdrawal that would overdraw an account can be covered by linked overdraft protection, waiving the fee on an account's first use each month rather than penalizing an occasional slip-up.
  • Keeps the customer informed — a statement is produced for every account, showing the current balance and every transaction behind it.
  • Feeds the rest of the bank — account and transaction data is unloaded into DB2 every night, so reporting, compliance, and other downstream systems are working from the same numbers the account-servicing programs just produced.

How the system is organized

No direct program-to-program calls among the six COBOL programs — the two shared files are the only thing tying them together. The PL/I program stands apart entirely.

  • Online, any order — ACCTINQ (balance lookup, read-only), DEPOSIT, WITHDRWL. Each opens ACCOUNT-FILE by account ID; DEPOSIT and WITHDRWL also update it and append to TRANSACTION-FILE.
  • Nightly batch, fixed order (DPBATCH.jcl) — STEP010 ACCTMSTR (dormancy, closure, fee codes, monthly fees, control totals), STEP020 INTCALC (interest), STEP030 STMTGEN (statements). Each step reads ACCOUNT-FILE sequentially, one account after another.
  • Shared files — ACCOUNT-FILE (VSAM, keyed by account ID) and TRANSACTION-FILE (VSAM, a sequential log) are the only state the six COBOL programs have in common.
  • Downstream — a final batch step unloads both files into DB2 as ACCOUNT_MASTER and TRANSACTION_LOG.
  • PL/I companion, separate — OVRDRAFT runs on its own, with its own request/response files, and isn't wired into the COBOL programs' shared state in this fixture.

ACCTINQ

Online / ad hoc

Read-only account lookup

The simplest program in the module. Looks up one account by ID and displays its balance and status. Never writes to any file.

4/4 paragraphs · 100% coverage · 3 rules extracted

  • src/ACCTINQ.cbl:1000-READ-ACCOUNTWhen a customer account inquiry is requested, the system looks up the account record; if no matching account is found, it displays an "ACCOUNT NOT FOUND" message and does not display any account details.
  • src/ACCTINQ.cbl:1000-READ-ACCOUNTThis account inquiry function is read-only: it only displays the current balance and status, and never updates account information.
  • src/ACCTINQ.cbl:2000-DISPLAY-BALANCEWhen the account is found, the inquiry result displayed to the user includes the account identifier, the current account balance, and the account status.

DEPOSIT

Online / ad hoc

Posts a deposit

Posts a deposit to an account: rejects deposits to closed accounts, reactivates dormant ones, applies the monthly maintenance fee waiver at a $500 balance threshold, and logs the transaction.

8/8 paragraphs · 100% coverage · 10 rules extracted

  • src/DEPOSIT.cbl:2000-READ-ACCOUNTIf the account number on the deposit cannot be found in the account file, the deposit is rejected.
  • src/DEPOSIT.cbl:2100-VALIDATE-ACCOUNTA deposit is rejected if the account status is closed.
  • src/DEPOSIT.cbl:2100-VALIDATE-ACCOUNTIf the account is dormant (and not closed), a deposit automatically reactivates the account to active status.
  • src/DEPOSIT.cbl:3000-POST-DEPOSITThe deposit amount is added to the account's current balance.
  • src/DEPOSIT.cbl:3000-POST-DEPOSITWhen a deposit is posted, the account's last-transaction date is updated to the current date.
  • src/DEPOSIT.cbl:3100-CHECK-FEE-WAIVERIf the account balance after the deposit is at or above the fee-waiver threshold, the monthly maintenance fee is waived for this account.
  • src/DEPOSIT.cbl:3100-CHECK-FEE-WAIVERIf the account balance after the deposit is below the fee-waiver threshold, a monthly maintenance fee is immediately deducted from the account balance.
  • src/DEPOSIT.cbl:4000-WRITE-TRANSACTIONEvery accepted deposit is recorded as a transaction log entry showing the account number, a transaction type of deposit, the deposit amount, the resulting account balance, and the date of the transaction.
  • src/DEPOSIT.cbl:4000-WRITE-TRANSACTIONWhen a deposit is accepted, the account's stored record is updated to reflect the new balance and status.
  • src/DEPOSIT.cbl:0000-MAINIf the account fails validation (not found or closed), no posting, fee check, or transaction logging occurs for that deposit.

WITHDRWL

Online / ad hoc

Posts a withdrawal

Posts a withdrawal: rejects closed or dormant accounts, enforces a $100 overdraft cushion, and logs the transaction.

8/8 paragraphs · 100% coverage · 5 rules extracted

  • src/WITHDRWL.cbl:2000-READ-ACCOUNTA withdrawal request is rejected if the account cannot be found.
  • src/WITHDRWL.cbl:2100-VALIDATE-STATUSA withdrawal is rejected if the account is closed or dormant; a dormant account must be reactivated with a deposit before a withdrawal can be made.
  • src/WITHDRWL.cbl:2200-VALIDATE-FUNDSA withdrawal is rejected if completing it would drop the account balance below a required $100.00 overdraft cushion.
  • src/WITHDRWL.cbl:3000-POST-WITHDRAWALWhen a withdrawal is approved, the withdrawal amount is deducted from the account balance and the account's last transaction date is updated to the current date.
  • src/WITHDRWL.cbl:4000-WRITE-TRANSACTIONAn approved withdrawal is recorded as a transaction record showing the account id, a transaction type of withdrawal, the withdrawal amount, the resulting account balance, and the transaction date, and the account's record is updated to reflect the new balance.

INTCALC

Nightly batch · STEP020

Monthly interest calculation

Calculates and posts monthly interest for active savings accounts only. Balances of $10,000 or more earn a bonus rate; smaller balances earn the account's own stored rate — which physically shares storage with the checking-account fee code via REDEFINES.

9/9 paragraphs · 100% coverage · 8 rules extracted

  • src/INTCALC.cbl:2100-CHECK-ELIGIBLEInterest is only calculated for accounts that are both a savings account type and currently active; checking accounts, dormant accounts, and closed accounts are not eligible to earn interest.
  • src/INTCALC.cbl:2200-APPLY-RATE-TIERIf an eligible account's balance is $10,000.00 or more, the account earns a bonus annual interest rate of 4.5% instead of its normally stored interest rate.
  • src/INTCALC.cbl:2200-APPLY-RATE-TIERIf an eligible account's balance is below $10,000.00, the account earns interest at its individually stored interest rate rather than the bonus rate.
  • src/INTCALC.cbl:2300-COMPUTE-INTERESTMonthly interest earned on an eligible account is calculated as the account's balance multiplied by its applicable annual interest rate, divided by 12, rounded to the nearest cent.
  • src/INTCALC.cbl:3000-POST-INTERESTThe calculated monthly interest is added to the account's balance, crediting the account.
  • src/INTCALC.cbl:3000-POST-INTERESTWhen interest is posted to an account, the account's last transaction date is updated to the current processing date.
  • src/INTCALC.cbl:4000-WRITE-TRANSACTIONEach interest payment is recorded as a transaction history entry showing the account number, a transaction type of 'IN' (interest), the interest amount credited, the resulting account balance after posting, and the date of posting.
  • src/INTCALC.cbl:4000-WRITE-TRANSACTIONThe account's master record is updated to reflect the new balance and last transaction date whenever interest is posted.

ACCTMSTR

Nightly batch · STEP010

Account maintenance — dormancy, fees, closure

The largest and most complex program. Marks accounts dormant after 180 days of inactivity, closes them after 365, assigns checking-account fee codes, applies the monthly maintenance fee (at a threshold that quietly drifted from DEPOSIT's version), and produces the batch's control totals. Also contains two legacy paragraphs that nothing calls anymore.

20/20 paragraphs · 100% coverage · 13 rules extracted

  • src/ACCTMSTR.cbl:2100-READ-NEXT-ACCOUNTOnly Savings and Checking are recognized account types; any other account type is logged as a data exception but the account still continues through processing.
  • src/ACCTMSTR.cbl:2200-VALIDATE-ACCOUNT-TYPEWhen an account's type is neither Savings nor Checking, the system displays a data exception message identifying the account, but does not stop processing it.
  • src/ACCTMSTR.cbl:3100-CALC-DAYS-SINCE-LAST-TRANThe number of days since an account's last transaction is calculated by comparing today's date to the account's last transaction date.
  • src/ACCTMSTR.cbl:3200-MARK-DORMANTAn active account with no transaction activity for more than 180 days is automatically reclassified as dormant.
  • src/ACCTMSTR.cbl:3300-CLOSE-LONG-DORMANTA dormant account with no transaction activity for more than 365 days is automatically closed by the system.
  • src/ACCTMSTR.cbl:3400-SET-FEE-CODEFor checking accounts, a fee code is assigned based on balance: accounts with a negative balance get an overdraft fee code, while accounts with a zero or positive balance get the standard fee code.
  • src/ACCTMSTR.cbl:3400-SET-FEE-CODEThe fee code must only ever be set for checking accounts; savings accounts share the same stored data location for their interest rate, so setting a fee code on a savings account would corrupt its recorded interest rate.
  • src/ACCTMSTR.cbl:4100-CHECK-FEE-WAIVER-MSTRThe monthly maintenance fee is waived if the account balance is more than $500.00; if the balance is $500.00 or less, the fee is applied.
  • src/ACCTMSTR.cbl:4000-MONTHLY-FEE-PROCESSINGMonthly maintenance fee waiver checking is only performed for accounts that are currently active (not dormant or closed).
  • src/ACCTMSTR.cbl:4200-APPLY-MONTHLY-FEEWhen a monthly maintenance fee is applied, it is deducted directly from the account's balance.
  • src/ACCTMSTR.cbl:4300-WRITE-FEE-TRANSACTIONEvery time a monthly maintenance fee is applied, a fee transaction record is created showing the account, the fee amount, the resulting balance, and the date.
  • src/ACCTMSTR.cbl:5100-UPDATE-DORMANT-COUNTERThe account record keeps a running count of days since the last transaction, updated each processing cycle.
  • src/ACCTMSTR.cbl:7000-PRODUCE-CONTROL-TOTALSAt the end of each run, a control report is produced summarizing the total accounts processed, the number newly marked dormant, the number closed, and the number of monthly fees charged.

Excluded as dead code: 4700-LEGACY-OVERDRAFT-CHARGE, 4750-LEGACY-OVERDRAFT-NOTIFY — present in source, never reached from the entry point, so never handed to the extraction agent.

STMTGEN

Nightly batch · STEP030

Statement generation

Reads each account and its transactions and writes a printed statement. Opens both input files read-only, so its own 'refresh the last-activity date' logic never actually persists to disk.

7/7 paragraphs · 100% coverage · 4 rules extracted

  • src/STMTGEN.cbl:3000-PRINT-HEADEREach customer statement begins with a header line showing the account identifier and the current account balance.
  • src/STMTGEN.cbl:4000-PRINT-TRANSACTIONSA statement lists only the transactions that belong to the account currently being statemented (i.e., transactions are matched to the account by account ID).
  • src/STMTGEN.cbl:4000-PRINT-TRANSACTIONSEach listed transaction line on the statement shows the transaction type, the transaction amount, and the transaction date.
  • src/STMTGEN.cbl:5000-UPDATE-LAST-STMT-DATEWhenever a statement is generated for an account, the account's last-activity date is updated to the current date, regardless of whether any customer transactions occurred during the statement period.

OVRDRAFT

Online / ad hoc · PL/I

Overdraft protection decision

A companion subsystem written in PL/I rather than COBOL — real mainframe estates mix languages like this routinely. Decides whether a withdrawal that would overdraw an account can be covered by linked overdraft protection: denies outright if nothing is linked, checks the request against the linked limit, and waives the fee on the account's first use of the month. Runs independently of the other six programs — it doesn't share their VSAM files.

9/9 paragraphs · 100% coverage · 7 rules extracted

  • src/OVRDRAFT.pli:INIT_PROCEvery overdraft protection request is defaulted to "denied" and "no link" before processing begins, so that if processing fails partway through, the request is never mistakenly approved.
  • src/OVRDRAFT.pli:VALIDATE_LINKIf an account has no overdraft protection linked to it, the overdraft request is denied immediately without ever checking an overdraft limit.
  • src/OVRDRAFT.pli:CHECK_LIMITAn overdraft request is approved only if the requested amount does not exceed the account's linked overdraft limit; otherwise it is denied.
  • src/OVRDRAFT.pli:CALC_FEEThe first use of overdraft protection by an account in a calendar month is free of charge.
  • src/OVRDRAFT.pli:CALC_FEEEvery overdraft protection use after the first one in the same calendar month incurs a flat fee.
  • src/OVRDRAFT.pli:APPLY_FEEEach time an overdraft fee is calculated and applied, the account's count of overdraft protection uses for the month is increased by one.
  • src/OVRDRAFT.pli:WRITE_RESPONSEThe response to an overdraft request always includes the approval decision and the fee amount, even when the fee is zero.

Excluded as dead code: LEGACY_FLAT_FEE — present in source, never reached from the entry point, so never handed to the extraction agent.
